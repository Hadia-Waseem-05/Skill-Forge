About SkillForge

Why we built this platform

For Students

Enroll in courses that match your goals, then learn at your own pace through structured lessons. Track your progress lesson by lesson, pass end-of-course quizzes to lock in what you learned, and earn certificates you can show off to employers or peers.

For Instructors

Publish courses with a clear curriculum, build out lessons and quizzes, and watch your students learn and grow through the platform. SkillForge gives you the structure to teach and the insights to improve over time.

How It Works

Choose a course, work through each lesson in order, and test your knowledge with quizzes at the end. Once you complete the required lessons and pass the final quiz, you earn a certificate — a verifiable record of what you accomplished.
